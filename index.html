Wiring Legend

Live Wire (Red)

Feeds mains supply to the switch. When ON, switch output loops in parallel to the 'L' terminal of all 3 sockets.

Neutral Wire (Black)

Returns current from the 'N' terminals of all sockets back to the main neutral supply line.

Earth Wire (Green)

Connects all socket earth ('E') terminals together and leads down to the central earth grounding rod.

Operation & Parallel Testing
